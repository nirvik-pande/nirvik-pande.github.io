About me.

In the Nicomachean Ethics, Aristotle separated technē, the knowledge of how to make a thing, from phronēsis, the judgment of what is worth making. They are different competencies, and a civilization can be excellent at one while being poor at the other. My current motivation lies in developing both competencies. “Worth making” is never a general question — worth it for whom, decided by whom, checked by whom — and each of them takes a different skill set to answer, which is roughly why I study what I study.
